This is Aakash Varney, the news read by Ashish P. Abraham. Today marks Mahashtami, one of the most significant and sacred days of Navratri, the Hindu festival dedicated to Goddess Durga. The occasion is being celebrated with great religious fervour and devotion across several parts of the country. In West Bengal, Mahashtami Pooja and other religious rituals will begin early this morning. Preparations are underway for the sacred Sandhi Pooja at various Pooja mandaps across the state, including Belur Mutt. Meanwhile, following the Chor Bhagwan stampede in Kolkata last week, the state government has taken necessary measures to ensure effective crowd management and the safety of devotees at Pooja Pandals across West Bengal. In Gujarat, the festival is being celebrated as Navratri with utmost enthusiasm and religious fervour. All the cities across the state are celebrating the festival with massive Vrasgarba events. In Odisha, beautifully decorated Pandals and temples are drawing large numbers of devotees. Special rituals are being performed at Shakthi Pithas, while major cities including Bhuvaneshwar and Katak are witnessing festive gatherings and elaborate decorations. Durga Pooja celebrations have gripped Barak Valley of Assam with colourful illuminations and elaborately decorated Pandals attracting large crowds across Siltia, Kachhar, Sribhumi and Hela Kandi. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will visit Dushanbe in Tajikistan to present India at the 25th meeting of the Council of Heads of Government of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization. The two-day meeting will commence today. According to the Defence Ministry, the meeting will focus on strengthening multilateral cooperation among SEO member states, particularly in the areas of trade, economy, investment, connectivity, innovation and cultural and humanitarian exchanges. Home Minister Amit Shah accused the ARP government in Punjab of pushing the state into the abyss of drugs as a result of which numerous families have had to lose their children. In a social media post, Mr. Shah said he will meet the family members of those individuals who recently lost their lives for raising their voices against drug peddlers in Mansa, Sangroor and Gurdaspur on Tuesday. He said attempts to suppress the voices rising against drug menace will never succeed and the people of the state will deliver a resounding response to it. Maharashtra's first advanced Annapurthi grain ATM was inaugurated by State Food, Civil Supplies and Consumer Protection Minister Chagan Bujwal in Nashek. The initiator aims to provide food grains to eligible beneficiaries at their convenience beyond the fixed operating hours of fair price shops. Maharashtra has around 54,000 fair price shops serving approximately 7-7.5 crore beneficiaries under the public distribution system. Indian Embassy in Riyadh has advised the Indian community in Saudi Arabia to take due precautions under the prevailing situation and immediately proceed to the nearest safe place in case of aerial attack. In an advisory, the Embassy urged them to refrain from filming, publishing or sharing any information, photos or videos related to the ongoing events. The Embassy officials continue to visit hospitals where injured Indian nationals are receiving medical treatment and are in constant touch with their family and providing necessary assistance. The Asian Paragames 2026 began in Aichi, Nagoya, Japan with India aiming to build on its record medal hall at the previous edition. The fifth edition of the Continental sporting event will run until 24th of this month featuring competitions across 18 sports. The opening ceremony was held at Paloma, Mizu host to a stadium in Nagoya yesterday. Parabatminton player Pramod Bhagat and sprinter Simran Sharma are leading the Indian contingent as flag bearers. India has sent over 150 athletes to compete in 11 disciplines. The event will be streamed live on the Wave's OTT app and the Prasar Bhati Sports YouTube channel. In Women's cricket, India defeated Zimbabwe by 9 wickets in the second T20 international in Raya Port taking an unbeatable 2-0 lead in the 3-match series. The India Meteorological Department has issued an orange color warning for heavy to very heavy rainfall over Tamil Nadu, Puducherry and Karaykul till 21st of this month. Heavy rainfall is also likely to prevail over Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, Mahi and South Interior Karnataka during the next 2-3 days. And that is the end of this news bulletin.
