This is Aakashvarni, the news read by Amrit Bhushan. Today marks Mahashtami, one of the most significant and sacred day of Navratri, the Hindu festival dedicated to Goddess Durga. The day is being celebrated with great religious fervor and devotion across several parts of the country. In West Bengal, Mahashtami, Pooja and other religious rituals will begin early this morning. Preparations are underway for the sacred Sandhi Pooja at various Pooja Mandaps across the state, including Bailur Mart. The Mart will also perform the special Kumari Pooja today to mark the auspicious occasion. Meanwhile, following the Chorbhagans stampede in Kolkata last week, the state government has taken necessary measures to ensure effective crowd management and the safety of devotees at Pooja Pandals across West Bengal. In Odisha, beautifully decorated Pandals and temples are drawing large numbers of devotees. Special rituals are being performed at Shakti Peters, while major cities, including Pavaneshwar and Kattak, are witnessing festive gatherings and elaborate decorations. The celebrations reflect Odisha's rich cultural heritage and the enduring significance of Goddess Durga as the symbol of divine power and the triumph of good over evil. Durga Pooja celebrations have gripped Barak Valley of Asam with colorful illuminations and elaborately decorated Pandals, attracting large crowds across Siltra, Kachar, Sribhumi and Haila Kandi. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will visit Dushan Bay in Kthajikistan to represent India at the 25th meeting of the Council of Heads of Government of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation SCO. The two-day meeting will commence today.
