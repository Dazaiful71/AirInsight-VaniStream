This is Akash Varni, the news read by Ashish P. Abraham. Today marks Mahashtami, one of the most significant and sacred days of Navratri. The Hindu festival dedicated to Goddess Durga. The occasion is being celebrated with great religious fervor and devotion across several parts of the country. In West Bengal, Mahashtami Pooja and other religious rituals will begin early this morning. Preparations are underway for the sacred Sondhi Pooja at various Pooja Mandabs across the state, including Belur Mutt. Meanwhile, following the Chor Bagan stampede in Kolkata last week, the state government has taken necessary measures to ensure effective crowd management and the safety of devotees at Pooja Pandals across West Bengal. In Gujarat, the festival is being celebrated as Navratri with utmost enthusiasm and religious fervor. All the cities across the state are celebrating the festival with massive Rasgarba events. In Odisha, beautifully decorated Pandals and temples are drawing large numbers of devotees. Special rituals are being performed at Shaktipitas, while major cities, including Bhubaneshwar and Kartak, are witnessing festive gatherings and elaborate decorations. Durga Pooja celebrations have gripped Barak Valley of Assam, with colourfully luminations and elaborately decorated Pandals, attracting large crowds across Siltra, Kachhar, Shreebhumi and Halakandi. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will visit Dushanbe in Tajikistan to represent India at the 25th meeting of the Council of Heads of Government of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation. The two-day meeting will commence today. According to the Defence Ministry, the meeting will focus on strengthening multilateral cooperation among SEO member states, particularly in the areas of trade, economy, investment, connectivity, innovation and cultural and humanitarian exchanges.
