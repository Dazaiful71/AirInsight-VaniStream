This is Aakashvarni, the news read by Amrit Bhushan. Today marks Mahashtami, one of the most significant and sacred day of Navratri, the Hindu festival dedicated to Goddess Durga. The day is being celebrated with great religious fervour and devotion across several parts of the country. In West Bengal, Mahashtami Pooja and other religious rituals will begin early this morning. Preparations are underway for the sacred Sandhi Pooja at various Pooja mandaps across the state including Billur Mutt. The Mutt will also perform the special Kumari Pooja today to mark the auspicious occasion. Meanwhile, following the Chorbhagan stampede in Kolkata last week, the state government has taken necessary measures to ensure effective crowd management and the safety of devotees at Pooja Pandals across West Bengal. In Odisha, beautifully decorated Pandals and temples are drawing large numbers of devotees. Special rituals are being performed at Shakti Peethas while major cities including Puvaneshwar and Katak are witnessing festive gatherings and elaborate decorations. The celebrations reflect Odisha's rich cultural heritage and the enduring significance of Goddess Durga as the symbol of divine power and the triumph of good over evil. Durga Pooja celebrations have gripped Barak Valley of Assam with colorful illuminations and elaborately decorated Pandals attracting large crowds across Silchar, Kachar, Sribhumi and Hailakandi. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh will visit Dushanbe in Tajikistan to represent India at the 25th meeting of the Council of Heads of Government of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization SCO. The two-day meeting will commence today. According to the Defence Ministry, the meeting will focus on strengthening multilateral cooperation among SCO member states, particularly in the areas of trade, economy, investment, connectivity, innovation and cultural and humanitarian exchanges. It will also provide India with an opportunity to exchange views with other member states on promoting practical and mutually beneficial cooperation within the organization. In Punjab, eight workers of Vikasit Bharat, Giramji, including women, have been killed and several others injured when a four-wheeler rammed into them on Lehragaga Jackal Road in Sanguru district last evening. The victims were reportedly working on the roadside when the speeding car moved them down. Sanguru superintendent of police, Balvinder Singh Chima, told media that the injured have been shifted to various hospitals. He said the vehicle has been identified and the police would nab the driver soon.
